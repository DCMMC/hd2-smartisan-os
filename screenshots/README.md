# Screenshot provenance

- `desktop.png`: `logs/hd2-desktop-clean0.png` from the original workspace, real 480×800 HD2 framebuffer.
- `quick-controls.png`: `logs/hd2-tools-confirmed2.png`, active framebuffer slot confirmed at pan `0,1600`; 12 switches in three columns.
- `camera.png`: `logs/hd2-camera-normalboot.png`, the bundled HD2 Camera UI on a normal boot. The hardware preview Surface is not reliably visible through this legacy screencap path; still capture was validated separately by a 2560×1920 JPEG.

All captures were made during the Smartisan debugging session on the real phone. No mockups or AI-generated screen content are used. Framebuffer RGBX8888 bytes were converted losslessly to PNG. The phone clock and battery indicator are not reliable hardware validation.
